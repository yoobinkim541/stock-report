## Next Diagnostic Boundary

`discover_targets` matches compiled terms against page title, summary, tags and
verified aliases; it intentionally excludes compiled-source notes and child
pages. The public runner further limits candidates to unchanged public-derived
targets. These checks protect scope but the recorded candidate population is
very small. Current evidence does not establish whether the cause is genuinely
unrelated sources, vocabulary mismatch, surface/category exclusions or missing
eligible public topic pages. Do not weaken privacy guards or merge solely on
shared words to manufacture a successful accumulation example.

Investigate eligible public topic availability and vocabulary against retained
compiled terms before changing retrieval. Any model-assisted live reprocessing
must retain the existing public-only and daily-attempt limits. Existing legacy
titles also remain an explicit cleanup gap, not silently considered repaired by
the new-title generator.

## Candidate Availability Recheck

All 39 publicly attributable pages are on the market surface and have kind note.
Thirty-five carry the intentionally excluded `compiled_source` tag. Only four
pages remain eligible as reusable topics. Rechecking all retained compiled term
sets against the current four topics yields no candidate for the repeated Riyadh
airport, Houthi, Hormuz shipping and Ukraine energy-ceasefire source groups.
Thus a surface mismatch is not the observed explanation for those groups: their
related existing source notes are excluded and a reusable topic is absent.
Some terms are English while source/page headings are Korean; this is another
possible recall limitation, not a demonstrated fix from this inspection.

The routing model receives an empty candidate set and may propose new topics,
but its prompt does not require a topic seed for repeated sources. This creates
a cold-start gap: related source-note contents cannot establish the need for a
topic, and absent topics cannot become routing candidates. A remedy must keep
source notes immutable as source-specific syntheses, expose only permitted
related source notes as context and let the model propose a separately reviewed
topic with attributed claims. Shared keywords alone must not trigger merging.
